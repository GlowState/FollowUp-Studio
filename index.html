FOLLOWUP STUDIO GUIDE

How To

A quick, practical guide to the app, its features, and how your records are handled.

Getting started

  1. Open Settings to enter your business name, Google review link, and frequently used services.
  2. Create a Client with contact details, then add and schedule jobs from their profile or the Jobs page.
  3. When a job is complete, use the completion assistant to record it and prepare a review request or follow-up task.
  4. Use Snapshot, Calendar, Follow-Ups, and To-Do List to stay organized.
  5. Save an independent full JSON backup regularly, especially before updates.
Home Screen & navigation

Tap a Home tile to open its page. Press and hold any tile to enter rearrange mode, then drag it into position. Tap outside the tile grid to finish; your tile order is saved with the workspace.

The floating Home button returns you to Home from other pages. Keyboard users can open the rearrangement control and use Alt + arrow keys; press Escape to finish.

Accounts & cloud sync

On a fresh launch, the login screen waits for you. If your session is remembered, select Continue to open Home. Returning to an app that is still open keeps your current screen.

To use Face ID, a fingerprint, or a device screen lock, sign in with your password and add a passkey in Settings → Passkeys. On later visits, choose Use a passkey. Available methods depend on your device and password manager.

Safe demonstration: Demo Mode includes fictional clients and stores information in this browser only. Clearing browser data or switching devices may remove those records unless you export a backup.

Sign in from the welcome screen to access your private account workspace. Demo records are not automatically uploaded. Use a full JSON backup and Restore if you intentionally want to transfer records.

Cloud changes require an internet connection. Wait until the app reports that changes are synced before closing it. If an upload fails, this browser tab attempts to hold a temporary unencrypted recovery copy; a successful cloud save clears it. On reload, you can explicitly recover, download, or discard pending edits. If another device already changed the cloud workspace, download the recovery copy and resolve manually instead of overwriting. This is not a substitute for an independent backup; closing the tab may erase it. Simultaneous edits do not merge automatically.

Clients, jobs & archiving

Add a client without creating a job, or attach more than one job to an existing client. Their profile contains contact details, notes, and expandable job history. Use Edit to change saved contact details.

Swipe left on a client or use the profile’s Archive option to move that client to Archive. Archived clients can be restored there. Permanent deletion is different from archiving and requires a separate confirmation.

Saved services help you reuse common job descriptions; you can always enter a custom service. Jobs can be searched and filtered, pinned, or managed from the Jobs page.

Calendar, Follow-Ups & tasks

Select a calendar date to see scheduled jobs or add another one. Swipe between months or select the month heading to jump to a specific month and year.

The Follow-Up Center lists review requests, follow-ups, tasks, and scheduled work. Use its category selector to narrow the list. The alarm-clock Snooze action lets you choose a date and time; snoozing only changes the reminder.

To-Do List holds tasks you create manually or through the completion assistant. Completing a task does not send messages.

Find a Google review link
  1. Open the business’s Google Business Profile using the account that manages it.
  2. Choose Read reviews → Get more reviews.
  3. Choose Copy, then paste the link into Settings → Google review link and select Save settings.

If you are helping another business, ask its owner or manager to copy the link. Check that it opens the correct business’s review form.

Review requests & Social Studio

The review-request template can insert {firstName}, {clientName}, {business}, {service}, {city}, and {reviewLink} from the selected client, job, and business settings.

Preparing a review text does not send it. Texting opens your device’s Messages app so you can check the wording and decide whether to send it. Only mark a request as sent after you actually send it. Respect the Do not contact preference.

Social Studio generates editable posts from built-in templates without subscriptions, AI services, or API keys. It does not publish posts automatically. Only use approved project details and images.

Photos, backups & privacy

You can upload a business logo and small compressed client/job photo previews. Those previews are not a permanent, full-resolution photo archive.

Use Settings → Download full backup to export a JSON copy of your records. Keep it somewhere private. Backups are not encrypted and may contain contact details, notes, and images. The Restore action replaces saved data after confirmation.

This version is a beta. Before using real client data or selling access, independently review data security, photo storage, automated backups, recovery, and applicable privacy requirements.
